# Consent to be recorded — PRAMAAN research corpus

*Template. Print one per participant. Not legal advice; adapt to your institution's requirements.*

**Project:** PRAMAAN — face liveness and presentation-attack detection, built for MOSIP Decode 2026 (problem
statement 04).

**What we will record:** video of your face from a few cameras, under different lighting, while you look at the camera,
turn your head, close your eyes, smile and look at points on a screen. We may also print your photo or play a video of
you on a phone to test whether the system can tell it apart from a live person.

**Why:** to measure how well the system accepts real people and rejects fake presentations, including whether it works
equally well for everyone.

**How it is stored:** on an encrypted drive, never in a public place or code repository. Recordings are labelled with a
code (for example `P07`), not your name. Only the project team can access them.

**What we will publish:** only aggregate numbers (for example "BPCER 2.9%"). No image or video of you will be shown
publicly **unless you tick the box below**.
